# M03 finite-fuel propulsion — verified increment, 2026-10-24

**M03 remains In Progress.** The dedicated aircraft now consumes finite fuel in accepted physical steps, limits thrust when the last fuel packet is insufficient and produces no thrust when empty. Fuel removal updates mass/CoG/inertia while preserving fixed dry-body points and their velocities. This fixes the previous live body's unlimited powered thrust despite zero fuel. [Actual BEFORE/AFTER algorithms, SI equations, matched numerical results, rendered SVGs and graph explanations](../physics/aircraft-fuel/COMPARISON.md).

Throttle-off has no idle burn; startup/refueling, fuel persistence and water transfer remain unimplemented. The inherited flight/contact kernels are unchanged; only the explicit live authority installs the new resource-aware transaction. No new RPC or package is added.
