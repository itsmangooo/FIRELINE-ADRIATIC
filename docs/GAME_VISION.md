# GAME VISION — FIRELINE: ADRIATIC

## High concept

Persistent, community-hosted cooperative simulator of wildfire response on a compressed but recognizable Croatian Adriatic coast and adjacent Herzegovina. Players are members of volunteer/professional firefighting units, an aerial squadron stationed at Zemunik, and command/logistics teams. Incidents can start and evolve independently of any player; multiple fires (e.g., Split hinterland, Omiš and Ljubuški) compete for limited aircraft and ground resources. There is **no PvP, ranked ladder, classical match timer or forced match reset**.

## Unique promise

> Every firefighter, pilot and commander sees the **same fire** and works in a **shared living world**. Water and wind are not merely graphics: they change what happens to the terrain.

## Player fantasy

A player can remain on duty in Zemunik while awaiting dispatch. An alert requests aerial support near Omiš. The commander assigns one Canadair and a ground crew; the pilot performs checks, taxis, takes off, flies a geographically compressed 6–10 minute transit where applicable, scoops water, releases it into a crosswind, sees the water cover a *server-calculated* fire footprint and returns for another sortie, another incident or standby. Ground units meanwhile defend a settlement, deploy pumps and hoses, refill tankers and coordinate radio traffic. At night aerial attack may be suspended for safety while ground crews continue.

## Design pillars

1. **Simulation credibility:** aircraft, payload, wind, wildfire fuel and suppression matter. Unknown numbers must be labeled estimated and tuned.
2. **Genuine interdependence:** aircraft reduce intensity; ground crews contain edges and protect assets; dispatch allocates scarce resources.
3. **Persistent service world:** join/leave at will; incidents, inventory, vehicles and burn scars survive restarts.
4. **Community autonomy:** Windows/Linux self-hosted server, private/whitelisted communities, modded content, administrator control.
5. **Cinematic Croatia:** real-feeling karst, pine forests, stone/plaster settlements, islands, bright Adriatic light, dusk and smoke columns.
6. **Measured performance:** 1080p 60 FPS on an explicitly named target PC, with scalable presets and quantifiable tests.

## Fixed constraints

- Max 100 connected players **design target**; requires proof with simulated and human tests.
- No more than **9 operational aircraft** in the server fleet at once; configurable mix of CL-415 and AT-802/Fire Boss. Not every player is a pilot.
- Persistent incidents without session reset; 60–90 minute shifts/reports are optional operational windows.
- ~1:10 **distance compression for interregional travel only**. Keep physics, runway/water-scooping, town firefighting detail and vehicle dimensions in SI units.
- Zadar/Zemunik is primary aviation HQ; Split, Šibenik, Omiš, Brač/Hvar, Makarska, Ljubuški are later sectors. Cross-border operations are modeled as simulated dispatch permissions.
- **Server authority:** wind, ignition, burn stages, fuel, vegetation destruction, gameplay smoke density, aircraft/vehicle state, water footprint and suppression. Only cosmetic sparks, fire/smoke particle detail, splashes/steam and ambient VFX can be local.
- Organized unit membership and operator qualifications govern vehicle access; optional sandbox mode for learning.

## Modes and audiences

- **Operations (persistent):** 100-player target community worlds; dispatcher/incident commander/ground/air/logistics.
- **Training (offline/private):** aircraft flight practice, water release, hose/pump tutorial, no permission gates if server configuration allows.
- **Scenario (modded):** operator-configured incident, wind, location and unit assignments using same simulation.

## What is deliberately NOT in first milestone

Whole Dalmation terrain; photorealistic 9-aircraft fleet; certified flight physics; full 100-player server; real agency insignia; built-in voice provider; Steam Workshop publishing; hundreds of gear variants.

## Production success levels

**P0 technical proof:** single server + 2 real clients see same burning trees, wind and post-water cooling; late-join and restart replay succeed.
**P1 playable operation:** one coastal sector, CL-415, one ground vehicle+working nozzle, controlled incident and report.
**P2 community alpha:** persistent units and incidents, hostable headless build, multiple simultaneous sectors, measured load tests.
**P3 candidate:** full visual art kit, scalable graphics settings, qualified 100-player test, mod API, docs and moderation.

## Visual and legal identity

Use fictional in-game squadron and unit emblems (suggested: **Jadranska protupožarna eskadrila — JPE**, **Zemunik Air Operations**, **Coastal Ground Response**). Refer to real places/aircraft factually without implying real organizations have licensed, endorsed or supervised the game. No actual emergency procedures should be portrayed as official training certification.
