# Marketing and launch positioning

## Naming and trademark clearance

**Working title:** FIRELINE: ADRIATIC. Alternative test names: ADRIATIC RESPONSE, COASTAL FIRELINE, AIR & GROUND: ADRIATIC. Do not publish branding until Steam search, internet domain and trademark checks are completed. Use fictional player squadron names; no false impression of official government approval.

## One-line pitch

> A persistent 100-player-target wildfire response simulator where pilots, ground firefighters and dispatchers cooperate to defend the Adriatic coast from dynamic, server-simulated fires.

For public messaging always qualify maximum 100 players as *planned* until successfully tested. Avoid marketing real firefighter-grade certification or real-life tactical advice.

## Short store description (concept)

Coordinate firefighting operations across a living Adriatic coast. Join an air squadron at Zemunik, drive a tanker to a threatened village or command the response as changing winds spread wildfires across multiple regions. Every water drop and ground effort changes one persistent shared world. Host your own dedicated community server and expand the experience with mods.

## Target audiences

- Flight sim/aviation enthusiasts.
- Cooperative large-server roleplay and Squad-style command fans.
- Fire and rescue simulation communities.
- Croatian / Balkan geography enthusiasts.
- Dedicated-server and modding communities.

## Content milestones and proof-first marketing

**Phase A (feasibility):** devlog showing actual wind vectors, server fire state, two clients see same tree burning. Publish test screenshots with clear prototype label.
**Phase B (core loop):** CL-415 flight/scoop/drop simulator, comparison of wind and water footprints, genuine video from Unity client.
**Phase C (co-op):** first fire truck and hose + working dispatch; before/after incident AAR.
**Phase D (community):** closed alpha with self-hosted instances and mod vehicle showcase; profiling transparency.
**Phase E (release readiness):** Steam page, screenshots/short trailers and demo only after truthful end-to-end playable scenes.

## Media kit requirements

- 3–5 gameplay screenshots (Zemunik, airborne, water drop, ground crew, command map).
- 30–60s trailer showing **real in-engine gameplay**, not fake CGI advertised as playable.
- Art assets: original emblem, typographic logo, sky/wildfire key art not using actual agency logos.
- Community channels: Discord, dev blog, Steam updates, YouTube developer logs.
- Modding + dedicated server wiki, visible patch notes, roadmap with tentative dates only.

## KPIs (no invented targets)

Track: wishlist growth after genuine video clips, demo completion, server retention D1/D7, community hosting interest, bugs/incident per session, tutorial completion, active player-to-unit ratio, AAR review rates. Do not confuse social media views with demand until measured.
