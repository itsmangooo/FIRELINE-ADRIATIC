## Project-generated and existing content

Canopy/trunk/building proxies and static sea reference material were generated by project code. They are not imported game-ready pines, houses, vehicles or a hydrodynamic water system. Existing Unity HDRP starter content and packages keep their original Unity/package terms. No CL-415 model is included; its official source download still needs authenticated access.
